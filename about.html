Our story

Built by learners, for learners.

TargetBand9 started as a simple idea: IELTS prep should feel like real practice, not guesswork.

Millions of people take the IELTS exam every year to study, work, or move abroad. Most preparation resources are either outdated PDFs or expensive courses that don't tell you where you actually stand.

TargetBand9 was built to fix that. We give you exam-realistic practice across all four IELTS skills — Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking — with instant band scoring, so you always know exactly where you stand and what to work on next.

Whether you're aiming for Band 6 to study abroad or chasing a perfect 9, we're here to help you practice smarter.
